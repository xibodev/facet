# Character animation

Use this pack when the production has character artwork, rigs, poses, or
authorable illustrated scenes. The pack does not bundle a character-generation
model or guarantee a particular animation runtime.
Assess `character-animation` against the supplied assets and live operations
before choosing a renderer.

Confirm the available assets, rig structure, actions, timing, camera, and audio.
Choose a live renderer that can express the requested motion, keep poses and
layer order consistent, and synchronize movement to speech or music only when
requested. Review silhouettes, continuity, frame pacing, clipping, and asset
rights.
