# Social & Short-Form Video Pack

Use this skill when converting long-form recordings into viral 9:16 vertical shorts, reels, and clips with burned-in animated captions.

## Overview

1. **Source Ingest & Transcribe:** Load podcast, interview, or webinar video into `assets/`.
2. **Hook Detection:** Identify punchy 15-60 second narrative moments.
3. **9:16 Reframe & Crop:** Center speakers or important action into vertical format via `facet tools run edit`.
4. **Animated Captions:** Generate subtitle sync and burn-in stylized word-level highlights.
5. **Fast Pacing:** Trim hesitation pauses and add subtle sound design effects.
6. **Delivery QA:** Verify vertical framing, caption margins, and audio loudness.
